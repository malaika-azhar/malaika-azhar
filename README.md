## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **SOC Analyst / Network Engineering** professional with **3 months of hands-on SOC experience**, building practical skills across Blue Team operations, network infrastructure, and digital forensics.

My work spans two connected tracks:
- **Blue Team / SOC:** SIEM alert triage, log correlation, Windows/Linux forensics, and threat framework mapping (MITRE ATT&CK, D3FEND, Pyramid of Pain) — practiced through a Blue Team internship and a growing library of hands-on labs.
- **Network Engineering:** Cisco IOS routing & switching, VLAN segmentation, ACLs, and secure remote access — built and hardened in Cisco Packet Tracer.

I learn by building real systems and documenting the actual debugging path — including the errors, not just the clean result.
